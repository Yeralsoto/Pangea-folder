PANGEA VENTURES INTERNATIONAL · BRAND KIT
Founders: Erin Berger and Yeraldin Soto · Edition 02 · 2026

01 Brand Book              The full brand: story, beliefs, values, full cycle, asset types, markets,
                           services and rates, voice, Spanish messaging, logo, color, type, usage rules.
02 Logos                   SVG (for print, web, designers) and PNG (transparent, for Canva, Word, slides).
                           Two-tone is the primary logo. Use "reversed" or "white" on dark backgrounds.
03 Colors                  HEX, RGB and CMYK values, plus a CSS tokens file for the website.
04 Typography              Newsreader + Geist, both free on Google Fonts and in Canva.
05 Services & Rates        Two-page rate card to send to clients.
06 Company Overview        Two-page overview in English and in Spanish.
07 Stationery              Letterhead, print-ready business cards (with 0.125 in bleed) and email signatures.
08 Social Media            Ready-to-post images (EN + ES), stories, LinkedIn banner and avatar,
                           and blank backgrounds to add new text in Canva.
09 Copy                    Every approved sentence in one text file: taglines, pitch, values, services, bios.

Making new content: start from a blank template in 08, write from the copy in 09,
use Newsreader for the headline and Geist for everything else, and keep to the palette in 03.

Email signatures: add the logo image in your email client, then paste the rest of the signature.
