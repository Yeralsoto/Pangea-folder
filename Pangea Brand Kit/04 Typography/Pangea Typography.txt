PANGEA VENTURES INTERNATIONAL · TYPOGRAPHY

Display: Newsreader, Light 300 and Regular 400
  Headlines, pull quotes, recommendations. Download free: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Newsreader

Text and numbers: Geist, Regular 400 and Medium 500
  Body text, data, prices, labels. Download free: https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Geist

Both are free (SIL Open Font License) and available directly in Canva, Google Docs/Slides and Figma.
If a tool does not have them: Georgia for Newsreader, Arial for Geist.

Type scale (px): 12 / 14 / 16 / 20 / 25 / 31 / 39 / 49 / 61
Labels: uppercase, letter-spacing +0.14em. Headlines above 31 px: letter-spacing -0.02em.
Numbers in tables: tabular figures.

The PANGEA wordmark is custom lettering (the Λ replaces each A). Always use the logo files in 02 Logos. Never type it.
